I build, analyze and optimize computer systems. I’ve worked across the stack, spanning large-scale distributed systems, databases, networks, architecture and AI systems. I’m currently working on the compiler stack for AI.

Earlier at Meta, I’ve worked on systems and model optimizations, debugging tools and monitoring systems. At AWS, I was leading efforts in the databases and distributed systems areas. At Yahoo, I built systems in production for end-to-end real time performance diagnosis in large-scale content and cloud providers. I worked on the Yahoo video CDN, which delivered one of the largest-scale live events on the Internet at that time.

My work on AI was featured in the McKinsey Technology Trends for 2024. I have been invited by the Federal Communications Commission, among others, to present my work. Recipient of the IRTF ANRP award for contributions to networking field in 2018. I’m a committer and founding engineer of OpenSearch.

Open source software

Committer and area lead on Open Distro for Elasticsearch. ShaperProbe (traffic shaping detection and estimation), DiffProbe (traffic discrimination detection), Pathload2 (available bandwidth estimation) and Pythia (performance diagnosis) are systems for understanding end-to-end performance of Internet paths. They are hosted on my GitHub. ShaperProbe and Pathload2 were public-domain services that had millions of users from 2010 to 2015, and the former was a part of the FreeBSD Ports distribution.
